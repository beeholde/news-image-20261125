Entry for [Sum up 25 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261125).

A one-page broadsheet of Wednesday 25 November 2026, final full-day
edition: 48 stories across 9 regions, every one dated to that Wednesday
(UTC). The lead covers Washington's proposed tariffs on 14 economies;
three illustrated heroes follow — Shenzhou-24 docking at Tiangong with
the first foreign astronaut aboard, the Kospi's 6.16% closing rout, and
the Cairo Ring Road tanker fire — plus twelve cards, a 32-story
also-list, a UTC timeline, a dotted world map, and a DATE CHECK box
naming the big Tuesday stories that were left out on purpose.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the pre-rendered
`entry.png` (1024×1024, 210,645 bytes) to `/output/entry.png` and
verifies the copy byte-for-byte against its recorded SHA-256, enforcing
the 1 MiB cap. Running it twice writes identical bytes.

```sh
python3 main.py
```

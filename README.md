Entry for [Sum up 25 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261125).

A one-page broadsheet of Wednesday 25 November 2026: 28 stories across
5 regions, every one dated to that Wednesday (UTC). Three illustrated
leads — Shenzhou-24 carrying the first foreign astronaut to Tiangong,
the Kospi's 4.9% AI sell-off, and the Flinders Island bushfire emergency
— plus a UTC timeline, a dotted world map, the day in numbers, and a
DATE CHECK box naming the big Tuesday stories that were left out on
purpose.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the pre-rendered
`entry.png` (1024×1024, 195,752 bytes) to `/output/entry.png` and verifies
the copy byte-for-byte against its recorded SHA-256, enforcing the 1 MiB
cap. Running it twice writes identical bytes.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
